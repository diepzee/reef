---
name: whats-new
description: What the other people's assistants wrote to your spaces
argument-hint: "[since, for example 2026-08-01]"
---

Report what changed across this person's Reef spaces.

Call `catch_up`. If $ARGUMENTS names a moment, pass it as `since`. Otherwise
call it without `since`, and it covers the last 7 days.

Group what comes back by space and say who wrote what. Lead with the entries
somebody else wrote. This feed is for the other people's assistants, not for a
replay of the person's own edits.
