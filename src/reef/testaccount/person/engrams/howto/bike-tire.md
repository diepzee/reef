# Fixing a flat bike tyre

- Find the leak: hold the inner tube in a bucket of water.
- Let the glue dry for 5 minutes. Press the patch on firmly for 1 minute.
- Tyre pressure of my city bike: 4.5 bar rear, 4 bar front.
- Tools are in the box in the garage, top drawer.
