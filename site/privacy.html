Privacy

reef holds the things you and your people choose to remember together. This page says plainly what it stores, where that lives, who can read it, and how to get it back or delete it.

Last updated 11 August 2026.

What reef stores

WhatWhy
The pages you write — text, titles, tagsThis is the product. It is the memory itself.
Images you addAttached to pages you or your cove members write.
Your email address and display nameTo let you in, and to show members who else is in a cove.
An identifier from your sign-in providerThe durable way to recognise you. Emails change; this does not.
Page history — who changed what, and whenSo a shared memory can be traced and corrected.

reef has no advertising, no trackers, and no analytics of your content. It sends no email at all — not even for invitations.

Who can see it

Your personal space is yours alone. A cove is visible to every member of that cove, in full, including everything written before they joined. reef tells you this before you invite anyone, because it cannot be undone: removing someone stops future access, but it cannot unsee what they read.

Separation between spaces is enforced by the database itself, not only by application code, so a bug in the app cannot quietly leak one cove into another.

Wouter, who runs reef, has the technical ability to reach the database — as the operator of any hosted service does. He does not read your pages.

Your AI assistant

reef is built to be read and written by an AI assistant you connect to it, such as Claude. When you do that, the pages your assistant reads are sent to whoever runs it, and their privacy terms apply to that exchange — not reef's. reef does not choose your assistant and does not send your pages anywhere on its own.

reef does not train any model on your content.

Where it lives

  • Pages, accounts and history — a PostgreSQL database hosted by Railway, in the United States.
  • Images and backups — Cloudflare R2, in the European Union.
  • Sign-in — handled by WorkOS. reef never sees your password; it only learns your verified email address.
  • Traffic — served through Cloudflare.

Backups are taken so a failure does not lose your memory. They are encrypted at rest by the storage provider and are kept no longer than needed to restore from a problem.

How long it is kept

Your pages stay until you or a cove owner delete them. If you want your account and everything in your personal space removed, ask and it will be done. Content you contributed to a shared cove may remain visible to that cove, because it forms part of a shared record other people rely on — tell us if that is a problem for you and we will work it out.

Getting in, and getting out

reef is invite-only. There is no sign-up: someone already using reef has to invite your address.

You can ask for a copy of everything reef holds about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email wouter@rugvin.be and you will get a real answer from a person.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and you will be told before it takes effect.
