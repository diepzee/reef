How it works

Start a cove, invite your people

Your household, the school circle, you and the accountant. Invites go by email; whoever starts a cove looks after it.

Grow pages together

Memory lives in real pages you can open and edit — not a black box. Your assistant writes them mid-conversation; you garden them whenever you care to.

The app

Browse it like people do

Every cove and page is right there in the browser — read, edit, invite, export. What your assistant knows is exactly what you can see.

Yours, actually

Built to be trusted with a life

Private by architecture

Your personal cove is invisible to everyone else — enforced row by row in the database itself, not by app code promising to behave.

Sharing is consent

Nothing moves from private to shared without an explicit yes, and a shared cove always shows exactly who can read it.

Leave with everything

One export turns every page into plain files on your machine. Your memory outlives any service — including this one.
